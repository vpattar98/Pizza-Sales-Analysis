# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Project

## 📌 About this Project

---

## 📊 Project Overview
The Pizza Sales Analysis project examines sales performance, customer preferences, and revenue trends from January 2015 to December 2015. This interactive dashboard provides valuable insights into peak sales periods, top-selling pizzas, and order trends, enabling data-driven decision-making for business growth.

---

## 🧾 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

- **Total Revenue**: $817.86K total revenue, indicates the strong sales performance.

- **Total Orders**: 21k total distinct orders, show a steady level of consumer demand.

- **Average Order Value**: 50K pizzas were sold overall, indicating a high rate of product turnover.

- **Total Pizzas Sold**: $38.31 is the average order value, which gives information on consumer buying patterns.

- **Avg Pizzas Per Order**: The average number of pizzas per order is 2.32, indicating that customers usually buy more than one pizza at a time.

---

## 📊 Insights & Analysis

- **Daily Trends for Total Orders**: Orders peak on weekends, especially on Friday and Saturday evenings.

- **Monthly Trends for Total Orders**: The highest number of orders were recorded in July and January with the highest number of 1935 & 1845 orders respectively.

- **Sales Performance by Pizza Category**: The Classic category contributes the most to total revenue and orders, accounting for 26.91% of total sales and total 14,888 orders.

- **Sales Performance by Pizza Size**: Large-sized pizzas generate the highest revenue compared to other sizes, accounting for 45.89% of total sales & total 12736 orders.

---

## 🏆 Best-Selling Pizza Insights

- **Top Revenue Generator**: The Thai Chicken Pizza generated the highest revenue, accounting for $43,000 of the total revenue.

- **Most Ordered Pizza**: The total 2.5k orders were placed for the Classic Deluxe Pizza, indicating consistent client demand.

- **Highest Quantity Sold**: The Classic Deluxe Pizza was the most frequently purchased item, with approximately 2.3K orders.

---

## 📉 Worst-Selling Pizza Insights

- **Lowest Revenue Contributor**: The Brie Carre Pizza generated the least revenue, contributing only $15.6K to the total, indicating low customer interest or lower pricing.

- **Least Ordered Pizza**: The Brie Carre pizza received the fewest orders with 997 units, indicating that it was the least popular choice.

- **Fewest Pizzas Sold**: The Brie Carre Pizza recorded the lowest quantity sold with approximately 976 orders, reinforcing its minimal demand.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- **Optimize Inventory**: Due to the great demand, have enough stock of Thai chicken pizzas and Classic Deluxe & reduce inventory for Brie Carre Pizza which is generating lowest revenue, to minimize waste.

- **Increase Mid-Week Sales**: From Monday through Wednesday, provide special offers, discounts, or combos.

- **Focus on Best-Selling Sizes**: Promote large-sized pizzas in marketing campaigns, as they generate the highest sales.

- **Improve Marketing Strategies**: During the busiest months (July & January), target advertisements and promotions.

- To increase sales on Friday and Saturday, run special weekend promotions.

- **Menu & Pricing Adjustments**: Bring in new flavors to popular categories like Classic & Supreme.
